# August 12, 2026

We've simplified our plans. There are now just two: **Free** and **Pro**. Nobody loses anything. Every feature moved down a tier, and prices only went down.

### One Pro plan

The Enterprise plan is gone. Everything it offered is now part of **Pro**:

- **[Teams](/feature/teams)** — every Pro subscriber can create a team, invite members, and assign seats.
- **Per-seat pricing** — Pro is now **$29 / seat / month** ($24 annually). Choose your seat count at checkout and change it anytime from **Manage subscription**, with automatic proration. One seat works exactly like Pro always has.

### Formboard is now free

The **[1:1 scale formboard layout](/feature/formboard)** is no longer a paid feature. Every account — including Free — can turn on formboard mode, edit to real-world scale, and export to-scale panels for manufacturing.

### What this means for you

- **Free users**: you gain the full formboard layout. Nothing else changes.
- **Pro subscribers**: your price stays the same, and you gain teams and seat management. Add seats whenever you're ready to collaborate.
- **Enterprise subscribers**: your subscription has been moved to Pro at the much lower per-seat price, with the unused portion of what you'd already paid credited to your account. Your seats, team, and harnesses are exactly as they were.

See the updated [Plans](/pro-plan) page for the full comparison.
