# July 31, 2026

This release lets you connect an AI assistant to your harness.design account, so it can work on your harnesses alongside you.

### AI Assistants

Connect [Claude](https://claude.ai), [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) or any MCP-compatible assistant from **Preferences → AI**. The assistant signs in **as you**, so it sees exactly the harnesses you see — your own, plus anything shared with you or belonging to your team — and never anyone else's work.

Once it's connected, anything the assistant changes appears in your editor **live**, while you watch. An indicator shows when an assistant is editing, and **Undo** works exactly as it does for your own changes.

This is an experimental **beta** feature. Assistants can make mistakes, so review what they change, and create a [revision](/feature/revisions) before a large edit so you can roll back.

[Read more...](/feature/mcp)

### Example prompts

**Adding parts to your library**

> Add the full catalog of Deutsch DTM connectors to my parts library. Be sure to include required
> accessories such as wedgelocks, and include the compatible contacts.

**Adding a device**

> Add the Link G5 Voodoo Neo 4 ECU to my harness. Use the correct connectors from TE, referring to
> Link's documentation.

**Importing from Excel**

> Import this harness into harness.design. It was created in Excel.

Attach the spreadsheet to your message and the assistant reads it and recreates the harness.

**Support questions**

> In harness.design I have a connector with different gauge wires coming into it. How do I assign
> different contact sizes to a single connector?
