## <IconShare2/>Share

If you just want someone to see your harness, share it directly instead of sending them an export. A shared harness is always the **latest version**, viewers can explore every view themselves, and nothing needs re-exporting after a change.

Go to <IconMenu2/>**Main Menu** → <IconShare2/>**Share**.

<br />
<img src="/view/main-menu/share.png" width={400} style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />

From here you can either:

- **Copy the harness link** and make the harness **public**, so anyone with the link can view it.
- **Invite specific people by email**, so only they can open the link.

Shared viewers get read-only access. Only you can edit your harness.

<Callout>
See [Sharing](/feature/sharing) for more information, including embedding a harness on another website.
</Callout>
