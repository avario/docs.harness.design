# Student Team Sponsorship

Welcome, and congratulations on being approved. Your account is already flagged, so your Enterprise access is live. This page is here as an ongoing reference for everything you should need about your sponsorship.

<Callout>
    This page is private and shared only with approved sponsored student teams. Please don't share the link publicly.
</Callout>

## What We Ask of You

Student competition teams are doing exactly the kind of work we built **harness.design** for, and this sponsorship is our way of supporting that.

In return, we don't ask for anything specific. But if the tool has helped your team, we'd love it if you shared that where it feels natural. Whether that's mentioning it to a friend or teammate, talking about your wiring process in a forum or build thread, or simply letting others in the industry know what you're using and why, that means a lot to us.

And if you want to go further, we'd be genuinely thrilled. A logo on the car or rocket, a sponsor credit on your team website, a post about how you're using it. None of it is required, but all of it is deeply appreciated.

This matters more the larger your sponsorship gets. If you asked for extra seats, we're trusting that the visibility you described in your application is something you'll follow through on.

## Your Enterprise License

Your Enterprise access is active for 12 months from the date your account was flagged.

### Your Team and Seats

Your sponsored account is the team owner. Create your team from <IconMenu2/>**Main Menu** → <IconUserCircle/>**Account** → <IconUsersGroup/>**Team**, then invite your teammates by email and assign them seats. See [Teams](/feature/teams) for the full guide, and [Seats](/feature/teams/seats) for what a seat gives each member.

Your seat pool is fixed at the number approved in your application. The **Manage seats & billing** option in the app adds paid seats and isn't part of your sponsorship, so if your team genuinely needs more seats, email us instead.

### Renewal

To renew, email us in the final month of your sponsorship. As long as your team is still actively competing, we'll roll it over for another year. There's no need to fill in the application form again.

### When your sponsorship ends

There's nothing to cancel and no billing to wind down. When the 12 months are up, the account reverts to the Free plan. Nothing is deleted, you keep every harness and all of its data, and the paid features stop accepting new work. See [what happens if I cancel](/plan#what-happens-if-i-cancel) for the details, including what happens to your team.

## A Note on Disclosure

If you're listing harness.design as a sponsor on your team website, competition documentation, or social content, most countries require a brief note that the relationship is a sponsored one. Something like "sponsored by harness.design" or "on a sponsored license from harness.design" is all it takes. This keeps things transparent for your audience and protects both of us.
